Emotional Control

Recognising reactions early and responding deliberately rather than impulsively.

About Kristin

I've been on both sides of pressure.

I spent 15 years in General Management, Business Development, Sales and operational leadership across the GCC and Europe — making decisions, leading teams and carrying commercial responsibility in demanding environments.

Alongside business, competitive sport has been a constant part of my life. I am a certified Sports Mental Trainer, polo player and polo coach.

Those worlds look different. Mentally, they have a great deal in common: preparation matters, but performance is determined by what you can access when the pressure becomes real.

Today I bring both perspectives together in my work with athletes and leaders.

What changes when the pressure rises?

If there is a leadership situation you want to handle differently, we can start there. The first conversation is simply to understand the challenge and whether my approach is the right fit.
